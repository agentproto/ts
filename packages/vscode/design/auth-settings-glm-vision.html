Profiles & Reach

A profile is a named account you create — openrouterPerso, not "the openrouter provider". It carries a credential and an allowlist of models it may run, and it is the unit of billing. Every profile has reach: across the fleet of harnesses, which of its models light up right now, which could with one named step, and which are simply impossible. This whole surface is one board: who you are on the left, what you can reach on the right.

Pack-only harnesses take no profile — you pick a fixed model triple, not a wallet+model. Lives below the board because it's the same for every profile.
Profiles & allowlists grounded in list_provider_presets (which providers exist) · auth_profile_summary (each named profile: id, endpoint, keyStatus, models.mode/ids) · catalog_provider_models (the allowlist picker + live prices) · write via setAuthProfileModels. Onboarding leads with auth_discover_credentials (adopt a local login) then auth_profile_create. Reach is computed per profile × harness from each adapter's manifest (interface it speaks, whether it accepts base_url, routeSelection) and the packages/llm-endpoint pack registry on :8788 — never hand-set. No secret ever renders: only last4/fingerprint, or the self-refreshing flag.
