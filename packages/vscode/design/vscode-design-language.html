agentproto vscode — one design language for every surface

Applying the sessions/harnesses language to the four surfaces with the worst UX debt. Left = current behavior reproduced; right = proposed. Everything on the right is hoverable/clickable/focusable here.
The five rules: 1 · every action is a labeled <button>, never a bare glyph span — hover may reveal, focus always reveals. 2 · dots encode state; they are never controls. 3 · status colors are the muted trio (moss / ochre / clay) via theme vars — no raw #2ea043. 4 · a failure renders a state, never a silent empty. 5 · destructive hover = clay, primary = moss tint.
