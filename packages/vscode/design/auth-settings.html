INCOMPATIBLEcodex × openrouterPerso

codex speaks OpenAI and accepts no base_url — its route is fixed to the openai preset in the manifest. That means openrouterPerso can never reach it, with or without the router: there is no re-pointable endpoint for the router to intercept. This is different from every card above — it doesn't move to READY NOW no matter what you enable. codex stays on openaiCodex → gpt-5.3-codex, native, and that's the only profile it will ever see.

READY NOW = profile approved for this harness's models (auth_profile_summary ∩ catalog_provider_models) and reachable under the harness's manifest (native interface match, or local router already running). COULD ENABLE = compatible in principle but blocked on one concrete gap — an inline toggle (router pack, model approval) or a guided flow (new profile, new pack). INCOMPATIBLE = no base_url in the manifest, so no gap exists for the router to close. Write path: setAuthProfileModels, auth_profile_create.
