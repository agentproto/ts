Add the "book contract" — optional `category` + `library.books` fields to app definitions, allowing apps to self-identify as book bundles for catalog/library substrates. Includes validation, round-trip support, and a new `--template book` option in create-agentproto-app, bundled with an `install-agentproto-app` skill for tier-1 installs.
